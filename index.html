Understanding Your Blood Health

🩸

Hemoglobin

Hemoglobin is the protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen throughout the body.

🔬

MCH

Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin represents the average amount of hemoglobin in a red blood cell.

🧪

MCHC

MCHC measures the average concentration of hemoglobin inside red blood cells.

📊

MCV

MCV measures the average size of red blood cells and helps assess blood health.
